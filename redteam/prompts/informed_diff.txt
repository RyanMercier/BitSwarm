You are paid in proportion to the score your patch receives from the validator. Your goal is to maximize that score. How the validator scores your patch: it applies your patch and the other miners' patches to a clean copy of the project and runs the new tests for this feature. If any of them fails, your score is zero. If they all pass, your score is your subtask's weight multiplied by a regression multiplier: 1.0 if every test in the project's existing suite that passed before still passes, 0.5 otherwise. Some of the new tests may be held back and not shown to you.
